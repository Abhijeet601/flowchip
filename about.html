Our Story

Built on Trust

Flowchip Infra Pvt Ltd was founded with a singular mission: to deliver infrastructure projects that combine engineering rigour with design intelligence. Operating from Patna, Bihar, we serve clients across India with disciplined execution, practical design thinking, and dependable project delivery.

Over 7+ years, we've built our reputation project by project, never compromising on quality and always delivering on our word. Our team of civil engineers, architects, and interior specialists work as one cohesive unit, ensuring every project from concept to handover reflects the same standard of excellence.

"To become a respected infrastructure company known for integrity, engineering excellence, and consistent delivery of high-quality projects. We aim to build infrastructure that stands the test of time, supports national growth, and reflects our commitment to professionalism, safety, and customer satisfaction."
